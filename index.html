About Me

Academic Identity & Professional Direction

I am a Master's student in Teaching English to Speakers of Other Languages (TESOL) at the University of Dhaka. My academic journey is centred on language, education, teaching, learning and research.

I have a background in education through my B.Ed. degree and practical experience as a trainee teacher. Alongside teaching practice, I am developing myself as an educational researcher with interests in language learning, English language teaching, assessment and educational issues.

I believe effective education should help learners communicate confidently, think critically, participate actively and develop as independent learners.

My Vision

To contribute to a more inclusive, effective, learner-centred and research-informed education system.

My Mission

  • Develop as an effective English language teacher.
  • Conduct meaningful and ethical educational research.
  • Connect educational theory with classroom practice.
  • Promote learner-centred teaching.
  • Continuously improve academic and professional skills.

Teaching Philosophy & Practice

My Teaching Philosophy

I believe effective teaching should be learner-centred, interactive, inclusive and connected to learners' real experiences.

A teacher's role is not only to provide information but also to create an environment where learners can participate, communicate, ask questions, make mistakes, receive feedback and gradually become independent learners.
